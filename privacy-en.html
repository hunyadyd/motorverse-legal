2. What data we process

Your account

  • Name, username, email address and password.
  • Phone number, if you add one. It is shown on your profile only if you turn on “Show phone number on my profile”.
  • Account type: Personal or Business.
  • For Business accounts: company name, address, phone number, email address, trade register number, tax number, and your self-certification that you only offer products that comply with EU law (and when you gave it).
  • When your account was created, which we show as “Member since”.
  • When you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

Your profile

  • Profile photo or the preset avatar you choose, and your bio.
  • Your car’s make, model, year and colour. We use these to show parts that fit your car first, and the colour becomes the app’s accent colour.

Listings

  • Titles, descriptions, prices, condition and category.
  • Photos and videos. Videos are trimmed to their first 60 seconds, and photos and videos are made smaller on your phone, before they are uploaded.
  • Location, at city level only.

Saved listings

  • The listings you save (the heart), when you saved each one, and its price at that moment. We keep the price so we can tell you when it drops, and remember which drops we have already told you about.

Communication and community

  • Chats with other users about a listing: the messages you send and receive, offers and counter-offers (the amount, an optional message and whether it was accepted, declined, countered or withdrawn), when each was sent, and when each of you last opened the conversation (shown to the other person as “Seen”). Whether someone is typing is passed on live and isn’t stored.
  • The users you have blocked, and when.
  • Reviews you write (the stars, the text, when you wrote it and when you last edited it), sellers’ replies to reviews, and your “helpful” votes on reviews. These are stored on our hosting provider’s servers. To write a review we check, on our servers, that the seller has replied to you in a chat about one of their listings or accepted your offer; we use your existing chats and offers for that and store nothing extra.

“Can’t find your car?”

If you tell us during sign-up that your car is missing from the list, we store the text you write and when you sent it, so we can add the car. It isn’t linked to your account. Only our moderators see it, and we delete it once we’ve dealt with it, at the latest after 1 year.

Settings

  • Notification preferences, app language, country and currency.

Face ID

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only receives a yes/no answer on whether the check succeeded; it never receives or stores your face data or any other biometric data. If you turn on Face ID sign-in, the app keeps you signed in on your iPhone and asks for Face ID before it opens; signing out then only locks the app. Your password isn’t saved for this. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device, with its key in your iPhone’s Keychain, as it is without Face ID. Signing out with Face ID off, or deleting your account, removes it.

Reports

When you report a user, a listing or a chat in the app, your email app opens a report addressed to us, and the report is also stored on our servers: who sent it, what was reported (the user, the listing or the conversation), the reason, the details you wrote, when, and whether we have closed it. Only we can see stored reports; other users, including the person reported, can’t. You can follow your own reports in the app under Profile → Settings → My reports: what you reported, when, and the outcome (for example “listing removed”), but not our reasoning, which is about the other user.

Moderation decisions

When we decide on a report, we store the decision with the report: no action, removing a listing, removing a review or a reply, or suspending an account; the reason we give; which of our moderators decided; and when. If we remove your listing, review or reply, it is hidden from everyone else and shown to you as “Removed” with our reason. If we suspend your account, we store the reason and the date, and show them to you in the app. Decisions are made by a person, not by an automated system.

Automatic word filter

We automatically check the text of listings (title and description), chat messages, offer messages, reviews, replies and profiles (username, name and bio) against a list of words that aren’t allowed, such as slurs, severe insults and sexual terms. Text with such a word can’t be posted: the app tells you which word to change, and the text isn’t stored. Text with a borderline word is posted, and an automatic report goes to our moderators with the field, the word, the text (up to 500 characters), your account, and the listing, conversation, review or reply it belongs to. A person then decides on it like on any other report; the filter itself never removes content or suspends accounts.

Support and bug reports

When you contact us from the app, your email app opens a message addressed to us. A bug report automatically includes the app version, your operating system version and your device model. We receive whatever you choose to send, along with your email address.

Technical logs

When the app connects to our servers, our hosting provider records technical logs: the time, your IP address, the technical description of the app and device sent with each request (user agent), and what was requested. We use them only to keep the service secure and to find and fix errors. They’re deleted automatically after [log retention period, e.g. 7 days].

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users see your public profile (name, username, photo, bio, car, “Member since”, and your phone number only if you choose to show it), your listings, your reviews and your replies (with your name and photo, to signed-in users). Your “helpful” votes are visible only to you; others only see how many people found a review helpful. Chat messages and offers are stored on our hosting provider’s servers and are visible only to the two people in the conversation: the buyer and the seller of the listing. Your list of blocked users is visible only to you; the people you block aren’t told, but they can’t message you. Your saved listings are visible only to you; sellers aren’t told who saved their listing.
  • Photos and videos of listings, and profile photos, are stored as files with long, random web addresses. Anyone who has a file’s address can open it, even without a MotorVerse account: that lets the app load them quickly. The addresses can’t be guessed and aren’t listed or searchable anywhere, but if someone copies and shares one, whoever receives it can open that photo or video.
  • Our moderators (the people we designate to handle reports) see open reports, with the reporter’s and the reported user’s names. While a report is open they can also see what it is about: the reported listing, even if it is hidden, and, if a conversation was reported, that conversation’s messages and offers. They can’t see any other conversation, and their access to a reported conversation ends when the report is closed.
  • Removed listings and suspended accounts: a listing we removed is visible only to its seller and our moderators. While an account is suspended, its listings aren’t shown to other users. Other users aren’t told why.
  • Business accounts: under the EU Digital Services Act, buyers must be able to see who they are buying from, so a Business account’s company name, address and contact details are shown to buyers.
  • Service providers who host and run the app for us, acting as our data processors: Supabase Inc. (USA), which hosts our database, files and sign-in on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (Amazon Web Services, region eu-central-1), and the sub-processors it uses for that. They may only use your data on our instructions.
  • Apple provides iOS features such as Face ID, the Keychain and notifications, under Apple’s own privacy policy. Notifications about new messages and offers are created by the app on your phone while it is open; their content isn’t sent through Apple or any other push service.
  • Authorities, when the law requires us to share data.

If any provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we only allow it with the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as an adequacy decision or the EU standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, listings, settings and votes: until you delete your account.
  • Saved listings: until you remove them from Saved, the seller deletes the listing, or you delete your account. Once a listing has been sold for 30 days it no longer shows in Saved, but the saved record (which listing, when, at what price) is kept until one of those happens.
  • Chat messages and offers: until you or the other person in the conversation deletes their account, and at most until 2 years after the last message or offer answer in that conversation, when the whole conversation is deleted automatically. When either of you deletes their account, the messages and offers of your conversations are deleted for both of you; the other person only sees an empty conversation with a “Deleted user”. Exception: if there is an open report about the conversation, about either of you, or about the listing, we keep that conversation (its messages and offers) so we can handle the report. It is hidden from both of you and only we can access it, and we delete it as soon as the report is closed, and at the latest 6 months after the account was deleted.
  • Blocked users: until you unblock them or one of you deletes their account.
  • Reviews and replies: until you delete them or your account. When the reviewer or the reviewed seller deletes their account, the reviews by and about them are deleted, with their replies and votes, and the seller’s average rating is updated. Exception: a reported review (or a review whose reply was reported) is kept, hidden from everyone, until the report is closed, and at the latest 6 months after the account was deleted. A review or reply we removed after a report is kept hidden for 6 months so its author can appeal, then deleted automatically.
  • “Helpful” votes: until you remove them, the review is deleted, or you delete your account.
  • Listings marked as sold: other users can still see them for 30 days after the sale (for example among their saved listings). The photos and videos of a sold listing are deleted 30 days after the sale; after that only you see the listing, without them, until you delete it.
  • Photos and videos you remove from a listing, a listing you delete, and a profile photo you replace or remove: deleted straight away, or at the latest within 2 days.
  • Support emails: for up to 2 years after the matter is closed.
  • Reports (users, listings, chats, reviews, illegal content, and the word filter’s automatic reports) and our decisions on them (the decision, its reason, who decided and when): for up to 2 years after the report was made, so we can handle complaints and repeated abuse; then they are deleted automatically.
  • “Can’t find your car?” reports: until we’ve dealt with them, at the latest 1 year after they were sent; then they are deleted automatically.
  • Technical logs: [log retention period, e.g. 7 days], then deleted automatically.
  • Listings we removed: hidden, and kept for 6 months after the removal so you can appeal; then deleted automatically, with their photos and videos. You can delete one yourself before that.
  • A suspension of your account (the reason and the date): until we lift it, for example after a successful appeal, or until you delete your account.
  • Trader information of Business accounts (company name, address, phone number, email address, trade register number and your self-certification): for 6 months after your Business account ends, for example when you delete it or switch to a Personal account, as the EU Digital Services Act requires (Art. 30(5)). We then delete it. During those 6 months nobody can see it in the app, and we only disclose it where the law requires us to (Art. 30(6)). Your tax number isn’t part of this and is deleted with your account.
  • Where the law requires us to keep something longer (for example accounting records), we keep only that, only for as long as required.

After you delete your account, we remove your data from our live systems straight away and from backups within [backup retention period, e.g. 30 days].

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. You confirm twice, the second time by typing DELETE. Deletion removes your account, profile, listings (with their photos and videos), saved listings, chats and offers (for the other person in each conversation too, who sees a “Deleted user”), blocked users, the reviews you wrote and the reviews about you (with their replies and votes), your replies and votes, and settings, and signs you out. Conversations and reviews tied to an open report are kept, hidden, until the report is closed or for at most 6 months (see section 5). A suspended account can be deleted too; the suspension is deleted with it, but our decisions stay with the reports they belong to (see section 5). It cannot be undone. If you have a Business account, your trader information is kept for a further 6 months and then deleted (see section 5).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access: get a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: have wrong data corrected. You can change most of it yourself in Settings → Account.
  • Erasure: have your data deleted, for example by deleting your account.
  • Portability: receive the data you gave us in a common machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Restriction: ask us to restrict processing while a question about your data is being resolved.

Email motorverse@gmail.com to use these rights. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.
