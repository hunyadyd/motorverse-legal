2. What data we process

Your account

  • Name, username, email address and password.
  • Phone number, if you add one. It is shown on your profile only if you turn on “Show phone number on my profile”.
  • Account type: Personal or Business.
  • For Business accounts: company name, tax number and address.
  • When your account was created, which we show as “Member since”.

Your profile

  • Profile photo or the preset avatar you choose, and your bio.
  • Your car’s make, model, year and colour. We use these to show parts that fit your car first, and the colour becomes the app’s accent colour.

Listings

  • Titles, descriptions, prices, condition and category.
  • Photos and videos. Videos are trimmed to their first 60 seconds before they are stored.
  • Location, at city level only.

Communication and community

  • Chat messages you send and receive.
  • Reviews you write, sellers’ replies to reviews, and your “helpful” votes on reviews.
  • Car of the Month entries (a photo of your car and a short caption) and your votes on entries.
  • Your votes on feature ideas.
  • Reports you send us when your car is missing from the list.

Settings

  • Notification preferences, app language, country and currency.

Face ID

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app only receives a yes/no answer on whether the check succeeded; it never receives or stores your face data or any other biometric data. If you turn on Face ID sign-in, your email and password are saved in your iPhone’s Keychain, on your device only, so iOS can hand them to the app after a successful Face ID check. Turning Face ID sign-in off, or deleting your account, removes them.

Support and bug reports

When you contact us from the app, your email app opens a message addressed to us. A bug report automatically includes the app version, your operating system version and your device model. We receive whatever you choose to send, along with your email address.

  • Business accounts: under the EU Digital Services Act, buyers must be able to see who they are buying from, so a Business account’s company name, address and contact details are shown to buyers.

  • Authorities, when the law requires us to share data.

If any provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we only allow it with the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as an adequacy decision or the EU standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, listings, settings and votes: until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages: until you delete your account. The other person keeps their own copy of the conversation.
  • Reviews and replies: until you delete your account or the review.
  • Support emails: for up to 2 years after the matter is closed.
  • Reports of illegal content and our decisions on them: for up to 2 years, so we can handle complaints and repeated abuse.
  • Where the law requires us to keep something longer (for example accounting records), we keep only that, only for as long as required.

After you delete your account, we remove your data from our live systems straight away and from backups within [backup retention period, e.g. 30 days].

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. You confirm twice, the second time by typing DELETE. Deletion removes your account, profile, listings, chats, reviews, replies, votes and settings, and signs you out. It cannot be undone.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access: get a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: have wrong data corrected. You can change most of it yourself in Settings → Account.
  • Erasure: have your data deleted, for example by deleting your account.
  • Portability: receive the data you gave us in a common machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Restriction: ask us to restrict processing while a question about your data is being resolved.

Email motorverse@gmail.com to use these rights. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.
